We are thrilled to share our passion for electric vehicles & sustainable motorsport.

We believe the base Tesla Model 3 Standard Range Plus (SR+, RWD) currently $46,990 to be the best car under $50,00, on & off the race track.

The SR+ is a lightweight, balanced ballet dancer compared to the brut force of the Model 3 Perfromance AWD.
