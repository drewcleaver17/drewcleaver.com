Prepared October 12, 2026, US/Central. Baseline: `5e00fe1e057eb230e511578ad47265aa3ac5c4f9`.

## Brief audit and editorial decision

The baseline had 423 published entries: 176 main and 247 supporting. Of the main entries, 59 carried the UFO reports & disclosure topic. A large hero, introductory essays, chapter map, specialist guides and the complete filter editor preceded the reading surface. This made a general introduction difficult to identify, especially on a phone. Pixel distances were not measured: no supported browser/viewport control was available in this session.

Useful retained anchors include human-origins evidence, observation and mathematical traditions, Egyptian and Mesoamerican infrastructure, religious manuscripts, physical and biological science, communication, nuclear consequences and the institutions supporting research. Repeating editions, releases, authorizations/effective dates, administrative renamings and later confirmations should remain distinct records without each taking a place in a brief introduction.

The released manifest selects 90 entries: 70 retained anchors and 20 bounded additions. Every original event, factual significance paragraph, source record, entity, relationship and work remains semantically unchanged. Original anchors and explicit journey/story/core/history/institutions/recent views remain available. No record was merged into an invented composite. New dates use supported years, spans and approximate/age conventions.

## Assessment and selection

The rubric separates impact (1–10: demonstrated reach, depth, duration and connections) from newcomer learning value (1–5). These are assisted editorial judgments, not measurements of certainty, goodness, fame or human worth. The explanatory `event.significance` prose and factual verification timestamps are unchanged.

A varied 12-entry calibration distinguishes fossils from origins, foundational mechanics from experimental confirmations, agency creation from naming decisions, ancient religious texts from asserted channeling, hearings from proof, and ancient tombs from modern excavation. The reasoning is stored in `editorial.calibration`.

All 443 entries have explicit review metadata: 221 assessed and 222 unassessed. The latter are not zero. Many retained sources document a date/action without establishing its enduring effects; each null review gives a bounded reason. Retained inspection scopes are inherited honestly; newly inspected syntheses and additions have separate source-access notes. No independent human reviewer is claimed.

Candidate priority is `0.6 × (impact−1)/9 + 0.4 × (learning−1)/4`. Changing the impact emphasis to 0.55 or 0.65 did not change the numerical top-90 boundary. The final manifest nevertheless includes eight deliberate choices outside that numerical set: Blombos, Epicurus, al-Tusi, Shen Kuo, James, Katherine Johnson, Pakal and Casarabe. These improve the introduction's perspectives, reveal collective work or explain a process without being credited with causing the larger history. Higher candidates including additional expansion observations, Mars life-testing, reconnaissance milestones and review/agency sequences remain accessible in full history.

The manifest stores stable IDs, rationale, question, version, review date, actual weights, sensitivity review and omissions. It is chronological and fixed. Filtering never replenishes selections. Newly imported records receive explicit null assessments and remain available in full history; changes to a selected anchor require editorial assessment review. The unattended dataset-only scope and external automation settings are preserved.

## Experience and compatibility

Fresh `/nhi/` defaults to Essential history, oldest first, empty search/date/facet restrictions. A short hero supplies Start reading and Explore all 443. A chronological preview precedes the filter editor; Start reading focuses the first actual entry. The primary view control is near search, result count and selected chips. Filter-group labels are visible and expand individually with selected choices summarized while closed. The giant desktop scrolling form is removed; a compact reading bar keeps Edit filters and View results reachable.

Each selected entry has a why-in-path explanation and a question, with evidence limits visible and sources inspectable. Scores sit in an optional editorial detail. Filters retain OR within/AND across facets, contextual counts, explicit BCE/CE dates and overlap, pending/invalid edits, subject search, chips, group clearing, URL restoration and focus recovery. Zero results retain restrictions and offer Search all history with these filters. Scope links preserve compatible filters; clear-all preserves scope/order/appearance. No impact dashboard or runtime ranking service was added.

No-JavaScript output contains the honest 90-entry chronology and a native expandable archive of the other 353 records, with an explicit full-resource link. It explains that URL filter queries need JavaScript. British racing green remains the default; saved Light/Dark choices remain honored. `/ufos/` redirects, people/profile links, sources, exports, aliases, anchors, directories and the searchable/off-menu discoverability contract are retained.
