## Editorial choices

- Maya describes people/civilization; Mayan describes languages. Yucatán is part of the broader Maya world. Living communities are explicit. Teotihuacan predates the Mexica empire.
- Indigenous contributors receive credit in the Florentine Codex. Colonial transmission and differences between Nahuatl and Spanish narratives remain visible.
- Brazil and Bolivia are distinct study locations. Brazilian lidar sites are named by state. Varginha and Trindade are Brazilian cases outside the Amazon.
- Pakal’s ancient tomb, its 1952 discovery and modern astronaut publishing use distinct anchors. The 1968 claim entry dates the original book; the inspected critique cites a 1971 English edition for its Pakal discussion.
- The Dresden copy is dated approximately 1300–1521 by its custodian. That copying range does not date the invention of its calculations. Long Count mythic origins are not sorted as physical creation events.
- The scholarly 1960 Proskouriakoff publication date takes precedence over a PBS chronology’s 1958 heading. The newer Getty project’s 1577 completion takes precedence over an older CONA catalogue’s 1578–1580 range; source notes preserve the conflict.
- Egyptian reign chronologies vary. Ministry ranges locate construction approximately; the physics abstract uses a different reign chronology. Unas is a rounded late-Fifth-Dynasty attestation anchor, not an exact composition year.
- A measured void does not establish its purpose. Amazonian predicted sites are not counted as detected or excavated cities. Similar monuments or symbols do not prove shared global origins or NHI involvement.
- Prato’s earlier historical webpage currently blocks retrieval. Its prior inspection remains intact. The new CMRI decision records disputed missing-material claims and official responses; it does not independently audit completeness or prove concealment.
- No complete Varginha inquiry, documentary, raw radar, original photo negatives or full scholarly book was reviewed. The Trindade case-card image was visually inspected. Abstract, catalogue, transcript and selected-passage checks are identified individually.
- No new YouTube upload was forced into the chronology. Original PBS transcript/episode and distributor links are more useful for this pass. The existing one-video-per-occurrence rule remains intact.
- New People profiles use the existing accessible initials fallback. No portrait attribution or license is invented. Profiles: Pakal, Ruz, Knorosov, Proskouriakoff, Valeriano, Tallet and Champollion.
