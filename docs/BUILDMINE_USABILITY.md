# Five-volunteer usability script

Status: protocol only. No volunteers have been contacted and no results exist.
Recruitment/outreach requires separate authorization. Invite people with varied
technical experience; participation and any showcase permission are separate.

## Introduction (read to each volunteer)

“Try making a personal site with Buildmine. You can use fictional information.
Please say what you expect to happen as you work. You can stop at any time.
Your private project stays on your device. We are evaluating the tool, not you.”

## Tasks

1. Start at `/buildmine/` without coaching. Choose blank entry or a synthetic
   PDF/TXT résumé. Add a name, about text and one piece of work. Skip any questions.
2. Change the style, try phone preview, edit a public field and explain what is
   private versus public. Check what requires review.
3. Save a private project, reload/close the page, then reopen the project. Verify
   that all answers and edits return and review approval is cleared.
4. Make a snapshot link, download a kit, unzip it and open the site. Locate the
   README, public site.json, editable source and publishing guide. Explain the
   difference between a snapshot and a permanent public site.
5. Follow DEPLOY.md to publish in an account the volunteer controls. They alone
   perform sign-in/account/terms steps. Never request their passwords/tokens.
   Record any inability to finish; do not count a local preview as publication.
6. Update a sentence and redeploy. Open About and Hello directly. Try contact
   links and mobile browsing. Optionally remove the Buildmine credit.
7. Ask whether they would keep using the site, what felt confusing, and what
   one change would have helped most. Ask separately before sharing any URL.

## Record per volunteer, using anonymous IDs V1–V5

| Outcome | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Background | Technical experience, browser/device, fictional/real content choice |
| Creation | Completed? Time to first usable preview; errors |
| Export | Completed? Kit opens; private/public distinction understood |
| Restore | All inputs restored? Assistance required? |
| Publication | Live URL checked with permission? Time; host; blockers |
| Update | Successful revised live page? |
| Intervention | Every hint and the step requiring it |
| Experience | Task failures, confusing wording, accessibility issues |
| Continued use | With separately agreed follow-up after two weeks: still live, edited, abandoned, or unknown |

Do not collect résumé copies, private answers, snapshots or contact information
in public issues. Report completion counts with denominators and separate
assisted/unassisted outcomes. Publication must be observed, continued use must
be confirmed, and unknown remains unknown. Five volunteers give qualitative
signals, not evidence of viral growth or a population-level success rate.
