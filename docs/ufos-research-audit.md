Research cutoff: September 19, 2026. This file is an editorial audit, not a transcript or an additional evidence source. The dataset links inspected supporting passages, identifies publication precision and distinguishes originals from reporting.

## Material corrections

- NYT online publication: December 16, 2017. Print front page: December 17. The original NYT page was inaccessible; contemporaneous ABC, Axios and WIRED material was read. Do not call that original inspected.
- The 2004 Nimitz encounter, later testimony and 2020 official video release are separate events. Fravor's inspected statement specifies November, so no exact incident day is invented.
- ODNI dates Navy standardized reporting to March 2019. Do not substitute a later press-announcement date.
- August 4, 2020 task-force approval and August 14 announcement are separate date fields.
- Grusch's reported July 2021 DoD IG disclosure, reported May 2022 ICIG complaint, June 5, 2023 public allegations and July 26 hearing are separate records. Confidential filings were not independently inspected. Procedural descriptions such as credible/urgent must not become findings that alleged craft exist.
- July 14, 2023 legislation is a proposal; December 22 enactment is supported by NARA's statutory text. Intermediate amendment votes and chamber passages were not inferred or fabricated. The law is not described as enacting the entire original proposal.
- NARA's September 4 (and later September 18) page-review date does not date an agency transfer. The eight-agency listing was inspected as current holdings only. April 24, 2025 has an actual release announcement.
- The historical-archive item is dated to accessible September 2, 2026 reporting. The SAM landing page did not provide readable notice text. July 31 is withheld; a notice of intent is not an award. No award is asserted.
- The September 14 waiver announcement concerns specified communications to designated PURSUE representatives. Reporting and a reproduced department announcement were read; the signed operative waiver was not. No blanket public release authorization or criminal immunity is asserted.
- Pentagon/war.gov/AARO originals that returned access errors were not promoted to verified primary sources. Accessible reporting is labeled accordingly. A reprint and reporting about the same announcement are not independent confirmation.

## Deliberate exclusions and limits

- A separate June 11, 2023 broadcast-interview entry awaits an inspected original or reliable accessible contemporaneous account. It is not silently merged into sworn testimony.
- The 2026 annual report is month-precision; reporting says released that week, not an independently established day. Its internally inconsistent component counts are omitted.
- Intermediate PURSUE release tranches are omitted rather than dated from snippets. The first and sixth releases have inspected reporting. The latest release's inconsistent file totals are omitted because the original manifest was inaccessible.
- New September 19 publicity around whistleblower appeals is not treated as a materially corroborated disclosure of physical proof.
- No private transcripts, internal task IDs, credentials or unrelated personal context are included.
- Hypothesis tests are editorial proposals, not represented as completed scientific experiments. Institutional conclusions are attributed and do not erase unresolved cases.

## Review ledger

Source-level notes explain access failures, reprints and unknown publication dates. The full initial JSON is committed with this audit; later material revisions retain previous summaries and Git history.
